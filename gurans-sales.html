NAME
gurans-sales — production enterprise sales management platform
SYNOPSIS 
gurans-sales [--role=1..6] [--approve] [--currency=NPR|USD|INR] [--report]
DESCRIPTION

A sales management system built for day-to-day use: sales staff log orders, managers approve them through a hierarchy, and finance sees reports and an audit trail. Heavy work such as notifications and reports runs in the background so the app stays fast.

FEATURES
  • -r 6-level role-based access control
  • -a hierarchical approval workflows
  • -c multi-currency pricing tiers
  • -n real-time notifications
  • -f financial reporting & performance analytics
  • -l comprehensive audit logging on an async task queue
STACK django 5.0 · postgresql · celery SCREENSHOTS

none — proprietary system.
